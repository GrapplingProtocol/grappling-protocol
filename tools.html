04

Fight-week check

A short readiness scan for the final stretch. Decisions first; improvisation last.

72–48 hours

Remove surprises

  • Use foods your gut already knows.
  • Confirm weigh-in time and recovery window.
  • Pack fluids, meals, and backups before travel.
Final 24 hours

Follow the plan

  • Check the trend, not every scale fluctuation.
  • Keep changes deliberate—no last-minute experiments.
  • Protect sleep and keep unnecessary stress low.
After weigh-in

Rebuild to perform

  • Start the hydration and sodium plan promptly.
  • Choose easy-to-digest carbs you've tested.
  • Stage the next meal before the first is finished.
