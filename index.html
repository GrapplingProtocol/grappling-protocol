The journey

How I got here

12+ years ago

First mat, first lesson

Started training at a BJJ school just down the road from home — no grand plan, just curiosity that turned into a habit.

College years

Stepped off the mat

Training took a back seat while I focused on finishing my degree in exercise science and sports nutrition.

The return

Back on, full time

Once school wrapped up, grappling moved back to the center of my life — this time for good.

The turning point

Watching bad weight cuts

Too many training partners were cutting weight the hard way — starving, sweating, guessing. I started digging into how it could be done better, supplementation included.

Credentialed

CISSN & CSCS

Earned my Certified Sports Nutritionist credential through ISSN and my Strength & Conditioning Specialist certification through the NSCA.

Right now

Finishing my Master's

Currently in graduate school, completing a Master's in Nutrition and Human Performance — still learning, still refining the protocol.

Today

Coaching the mat and the kitchen

I work with grappling and combat sports athletes, bringing clarity and accountability to their nutrition and supplementation. Off the clock, I coach nogi BJJ at a local gym.
